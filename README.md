# DeMitchell Technology Inc.

DeMitchell Technology Inc. is a Massachusetts-based applied AI company developing mathematics-first technology systems across FinTech, transportation, and healthcare.

Our work focuses on practical AI systems, quantitative modeling, software engineering, and disciplined prototype validation.

## Current Focus
- Applied AI systems
- Financial technology
- Transportation technology
- Driver-support research
- AI-assisted decision support
- Prototype-stage software development

## Development Approach
DMT emphasizes:

- Mathematics-first system design
- Responsible AI development
- Technical validation before broad deployment
- Real-world problem discovery
- Secure and private development practices

## Repository Access
Most company repositories are private while products remain in prototype and feasibility stages. Public GitHub materials are limited to high-level company information and non-sensitive documentation.
